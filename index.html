01 / OUR PURPOSE

Healthcare navigation is more than handing someone a phone number. It means helping people understand their options, work through practical barriers, and feel prepared to take the next step.

HOW WE HELP

From “where do I start?”
to “I know what to ask.”

Support should be understandable, respectful, and shaped around what matters to each person.

    ABOUT CAREPATH

    Built with the community,
    not just for it.

    CarePath is a student-led initiative exploring how clearer information and practical, nonmedical guidance can make healthcare systems easier to navigate.

    We believe the first step is listening. We’re learning from community members and local organizations to understand which barriers matter most and what support would actually be useful.
