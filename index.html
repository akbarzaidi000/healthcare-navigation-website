    OUR GUIDING PRINCIPLE
    ABOUT CAREPATH

    A student-led effort
    to make the next step clearer.

    CarePath is a student-led initiative exploring how clearer information and practical, nonmedical guidance can help people navigate the administrative and logistical parts of accessing healthcare.

    The idea is rooted in a simple observation: knowing that care may be needed does not always make it easy to find the right place to start. People can encounter confusing referral processes, uncertainty about costs, language barriers, transportation challenges, and difficulty understanding which organizations to contact.

    Rather than assume what a community needs, CarePath is being developed around listening first. The project aims to learn from community members and local organizations, identify gaps in existing information, and organize verified resources alongside practical questions people can ask.
